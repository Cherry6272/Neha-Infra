About Neha Infrastructures

Registered Class-I Civil Engineering Contractor in Karnataka

Established in 2007, M/s Neha Infrastructures has built a dependable reputation across Karnataka for civil execution discipline, practical engineering solutions, and transparent project administration. Operating under Class-I contractor registration issued by Chief Engineer & Commissioner (CE & CB South) Bangalore, the firm undertakes complex public works and layout infrastructure.

Guided by Proprietor Mr. M. Sundar Raju, who contributes over 32 years of personal civil construction experience, our focus remains on structural durability, site safety, and timely milestone fulfillment.
