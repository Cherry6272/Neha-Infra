Concrete-Lined Canals & Hydraulic Lift Irrigation Infrastructure

Lift irrigation schemes require specialized hydraulic engineering, mass excavation, watertight concrete lining, and heavy structural installations. Neha Infrastructures has demonstrated proven competency executing large-scale irrigation packages across complex Karnataka topography under major water resource entities such as Karnataka Neeravari Nigam Limited (KNNL).

Core Hydraulic Scope

Trapezoidal Canal Lining

Cast-in-situ concrete bed and side slope lining, expansion joint sealing, and seepage-loss prevention across long distance reaches.

Pump House & Jack Wells

Heavy reinforced substructures, deep well caisson sinking, pump pedestal casting, and high-pressure delivery chambers.

Rising Mains & Headers

Large diameter steel and ductile iron water pipeline laying, thrust block casting, surge protection manifold connection, and pressure testing.

Heavy Earthmoving Support

Precision canal profiling, prism cutting, slope trimming, and embankment compaction using in-house excavator and grader fleets.

Benchmark Project

Thimmapur Lift Irrigation Scheme: A flagship hydraulic infrastructure project in Karnataka covering 15 km of precision water conveyance, massive earth cutting, and concrete canal lining executed under KNNL.
