Thimmapur Lift Irrigation Scheme (LIS)

The Thimmapur Lift Irrigation Scheme represents a major hydraulic infrastructure execution delivered in Karnataka under Karnataka Neeravari Nigam Limited (KNNL). Designed to convey vital irrigation waters to arid agricultural regions, the project entailed rigorous civil construction across over 15 kilometers of canal corridors, intake structures, delivery cisterns, and high-pressure manifold installations.

Key Engineering Scopes

Quality Assurance & Execution Standards

Infrastructure works undertaken by M/s Neha Infrastructures adhere strictly to Indian Road Congress (IRC), Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), and departmental standard specifications:

  • Total Station Surveying: Laser-guided elevation and gradient control ensuring designated hydraulic slope and structural alignment.
  • Material Verification: Continuous cube test verification at approved government NABL testing facilities for all high-grade structural concrete and asphalt mixes.
  • Structural Compaction & Testing: Heavy vibratory rolling, core-cutter density verification, and pressure testing for hydraulic and underground networks.
