Thimmapur Lift Irrigation Scheme (LIS)

The Thimmapur Lift Irrigation Scheme represents a major hydraulic infrastructure execution delivered in Karnataka under Karnataka Neeravari Nigam Limited (KNNL). Designed to convey vital irrigation waters to arid agricultural regions, the project entailed rigorous civil construction across over 15 kilometers of canal corridors, intake structures, delivery cisterns, and high-pressure manifold installations.

Key Engineering Scopes

15 KM Canal Lining

Earth profiling, subgrade compaction, under-drainage layers, and cast-in-situ reinforced concrete prism lining to minimize hydraulic conveyance losses.

Heavy Intake Well

Deep circular RCC intake well sinking, jackwell civil foundation works, suction conduit embeds, and coffer dam dewatering operations.

Delivery Chambers

Elevated surge suppression chambers, hydraulic stilling basins, energy dissipators, and reinforced discharge outfalls into the canal network.

Cross-Drainage Culverts

RCC aqueducts, siphons, and box culverts crossing natural nallahs and rural road lines to maintain hydraulic continuity.

Project Execution & Quality Controls

Hydraulic civil construction requires continuous quality supervision. For the Thimmapur LIS scheme, our engineering team operated under stringent department inspection protocols:

  • Total Station Surveying: Laser-guided elevation and gradient control ensuring designated hydraulic slope without stagnation pockets.
  • Concrete Mix Quality: Continuous cube test verification at approved government NABL testing facilities for all high-grade structural concrete.
  • Joint Sealing & Expansion: Specialized bitumen/polysulphide joint sealants along canal lining panels to prevent seepage and structural cracking.
