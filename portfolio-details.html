Thimmapur Lift Irrigation Scheme

The Thimmapur Lift Irrigation Scheme is a landmark civil infrastructure project executed by M/s Neha Infrastructures for Karnataka Neeravari Nigam Limited (KNNL), Government of Karnataka. Valued at ₹12,221 Lakhs (₹122.21 Crores), the project has transformed the agricultural landscape of the region, providing vital water supply to thousands of hectares of arable land and ensuring year-round irrigation security.

The scope of this comprehensive project spanned over 15 kilometers of water conveyance networks, comprising major canal systems, high-pressure pipeline layouts, and large-scale earthworks. It stands as a testament to the firm's robust technical foundation, engineering precision, and capacity to deliver highly complex hydraulic projects on schedule.

  • Large-scale intake well and delivery chamber construction

  • 15 km of precision-aligned canal lining and structural works

  • Installation of high-capacity pump houses and electrical substations

  • Executed using specialized heavy concrete paving and earthmoving fleets

  • Over 1.5 Million cubic meters of precision earthwork, site grading, and canal excavation executed in record time under Mr. Sundar Raju's leadership.

  • Comprehensive hydraulic testing, structural reinforced concrete (RCC) lining, and trial run/commissioning completed successfully with zero incidents.

To support this massive hydraulic project, M/s Neha Infrastructures deployed its modern in-house fleet of construction machinery. This included heavy excavators (JCB & Tata Hitachi), Ajax Fiori self-loading concrete mixers, concrete vibrators, concrete batching plants, and soil compactors. The continuous logistics management and highly experienced project team allowed the project to proceed efficiently and meet all technical standards specified by the government department.

Mr. M. Sundar Raju's 32+ years of hands-on civil contracting experience was key to solving critical geotechnical challenges encountered during the delivery chamber excavation and pump house foundation casting near the water source.

  • Overcome deep groundwater seepage using continuous high-volume dewatering pump operations, guaranteeing concrete casting integrity.

  • Constructed premium quality RCC box conduits and drainage crossings to ensure absolute protection against seasonal monsoon floods.
