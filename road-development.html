Road Development & Improvement

M/s Neha Infrastructures specializes in executing professional road development and improvement projects under Karnataka municipal packages. We focus on high-quality asphalt paving, municipal road leveling, sub-base preparation, and surface dressing. Our fleet of modern machinery, including paver finishers and compactors, ensures speed and precision in execution.

  • Municipal Road Paving & Improvement Packages under Mukhyamantri Nagarothana

  • Robust Sub-Base Preparation, Sub-Grade Excavation, and Soil Stabilization

  • Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) and Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) Road Construction

  • Drainage-Integrated Urban Roads to Prevent Waterlogging and Pavement Failure

We have executed numerous road packages under CMC Sindhanur, Ranebennur CMC, and various town municipal councils. Our engineering expertise covers excavation, sub-grade preparation, wet mix macadam laying, and dense bituminous macadam laying, integrated with drainage channels to ensure long-lasting road surface integrity.

Key Projects Handled: CMC Sindhanur Road Improvements, Ranebennur CMC layout roads, and various municipal corridor development projects in Karnataka.
