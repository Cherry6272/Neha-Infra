Comprehensive Township Layouts & Composite Housing Schemes

Modern residential layouts and composite housing schemes require coordinated multidisciplinary execution—combining mass earth grading, engineered internal road networks, robust stormwater networks, underground utility conduits, and structural housing units. Neha Infrastructures delivers integrated layout construction for public boards and private institutional developers across Karnataka.

Layout Engineering Scope

Site Formation & Plot Demarcation

Topographic surveying, site clearing, bulk earth balancing, boundary retaining structures, and precision plot boundary stones.

Layout Road Networks

Subgrade stabilization, Granular Sub-Base (GSB), Wet Mix Macadam (WMM), asphalt surfacing, RCC box culverts, and concrete kerbing.

Water Supply & UG Drainage

Underground drainage networks, inspection chambers, storm drain side conduits, and potable DI/HDPE pipeline distribution mains.

Housing Block Construction

RCC framed composite residential blocks, brickwork, plastering, internal electrical conduits, sanitary fittings, and surface finishing.

Documented Layout Projects

Hiremalligewad Housing Scheme: Civil development, layout infrastructure, internal roadways, and utility alignments.
Gamanagatti Housing Scheme (Sites & Services): Site preparation, comprehensive earth balancing, drainage networks, and service connectivity for community development.
