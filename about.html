Company Profile

Civil Construction & Infrastructure Execution Across Karnataka

M/s Neha Infrastructures is a specialized civil engineering contractor established in 2007, operating from headquarters in Bangalore, Karnataka. The firm holds a formal Class-I Civil Contractor registration under the Chief Engineer & Commissioner (CE & CB South) Bangalore, enabling the execution of major public sector tenders and institutional civil contracts without ceiling limitations.

The firm is directed by Proprietor Mr. M. Sundar Raju, whose career encompasses more than 32 years of personal construction and site engineering leadership. His hands-on oversight across irrigation canal lining, stormwater alleviation, highway improvements, and institutional building projects has established Neha Infrastructures as a credible, reliable civil execution partner.

Equipment Capabilities

Owned Plant & Heavy Machinery Fleet

Direct machinery deployment ensures operational control, independent scheduling, and consistent structural quality.

Concrete Batching & Paving Plant

Specialized machinery maintained to ensure rigorous concrete mix design integrity and asphalt compaction across road and structural works:

  • Self-Loading Concrete Transit Mixers: Ajax Fiori units for on-demand site pours.
  • Stationary Concrete Batching Equipment: High-output controlled mix plants.
  • Vibratory Soil Compactors & Rollers: Steel and pneumatic rollers for sub-base compaction.
  • Heavy-Duty Paver Finishers: Precision asphalt and concrete laying.

Earthmoving & Utility Excavation Fleet

Heavy-duty excavation and site preparation assets capable of handling massive earth cutting, canal profiles, and trenching:

  • Hydraulic Tracked Excavators: JCB, Tata Hitachi, and CAT fleets for deep trenching.
  • Backhoe Loaders & Motor Graders: Surface leveling, grading, and embankment shaping.
  • Heavy-Duty Tipper Trucks: High-capacity material haulage and soil dumping.
  • High-Capacity Dewatering Pumps: Essential for deep stormwater box drains and lift irrigation works.
